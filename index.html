COVID-19

A TURNING POINT

From an idea to a small community.

During COVID-19, I worked with a small team to create a WhatsApp community connecting nearby villages with surrounding towns and cities.

We helped connect groceries from local farmers and producers with households that needed them. The idea was simple — create another route between local producers and consumers during a difficult time.

"I realised that being useful does not always require a big organisation. Sometimes it begins with noticing a problem and bringing people together."
